## Description
Into the Wild is a solo camping adventure where you survive in a procedurally generated wilderness. Start with nothing — gather branches and rocks, craft your first tools, and build a campsite from the ground up.

Explore a vast procedural world of forests, open meadows, rocky mountains, and winding rivers. Discover hidden caves with underground lakes, collect rare crystals and ore, and follow a mysterious trail of clues deeper into the wilderness.

Features:
- Procedurally generated terrain with forests, meadows, hills, and rocky mountains
- Day/night cycle with dynamic weather — rain, storms, fog, heat waves, and cold snaps
- Full crafting system — axes, fishing rods, torches, healing salves, and more
- Build and upgrade your campsite through multiple levels — fire pit, shelter, storage, crafting bench, drying rack, garden, log cabin with kitchen
- Fish in ponds, lakes, and rivers — cast your line and reel in the catch
- Explore caves with underground rivers, crystal formations, and hidden passages
- Cook food, dry fish, grow a garden, and manage your supplies to survive
- Ambient wildlife — birds, rabbits, deer, and fish bring the world to life
- 12 ambient music tracks with atmospheric sound effects
- Touch-optimized controls designed specifically for iPad
- Save and load your progress at any time
