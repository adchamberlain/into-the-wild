## Description
Into the Wild is a solo camping adventure where you survive in a procedurally generated wilderness. Explore forests, mountains, and caves. Gather resources, craft tools, build your campsite, and see how long you can survive in the wild.

Features:
- Procedurally generated terrain with forests, mountains, rivers, and caves
- Day/night cycle with dynamic weather
- Crafting system with upgradeable campsite
- Touch-optimized controls designed for iPad
