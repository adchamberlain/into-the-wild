Play Guide

Infinite Procedural World

Every playthrough generates a unique wilderness with forests, meadows, mountains, deserts, rocky cliffs, and underground caves to explore.

Deep Crafting System

40+ recipes across 3 camp levels. Craft axes, bows, grappling hooks, a hang glider, and more. Build your camp from a fire pit to a full log cabin.

True Survival

Manage hunger and health. Hunt rabbits, catch fish, cook meals, dry food for preservation. Brave storms, fog, heat waves, and freezing cold snaps.

Explore

A vast wilderness to discover

Wander through dense forests, open meadows, dramatic hills, alpine peaks, and sun-scorched deserts. Each biome has unique resources, terrain, and challenges.

Survive

Craft tools and hunt to stay alive

Progress from stone axes to diamond tools. Craft a bow and arrows to hunt wildlife. Build a grappling hook to scale cliffs. Cook, dry, and smoke food to keep your strength up.

Screenshot: Underground cave with crystals or desert oasis with palms

Discover

Caves, oases, and hidden secrets

Descend into dark caves to mine crystal and rare ore. Find desert oases with underwater gem deposits. Uncover the Explorer's Journal to unlock late-game content.

Screenshot: Developed campsite with shelter, drying rack, and weather effects

Build

From fire pit to log cabin

Start with nothing and build a thriving wilderness basecamp. Three camp levels unlock new structures, recipes, and equipment. Craft a smithing station, smoker, and kitchen for advanced gear and meals.

What's in the game

A deep outdoor adventure with systems that reward curiosity and patience.

6 Biomes

Forest, meadow, hills, rocky cliffs, alpine mountains, and desert. Each with unique terrain, resources, wildlife, and weather patterns.

Hunting & Fishing

Craft a bow and arrows to hunt rabbits and birds. Build a fishing rod to catch fish at ponds, lakes, and rivers. Trap small game with snares.

Dynamic Weather

Rain, storms, fog, heat waves, and cold snaps. Weather changes daily and affects gameplay — storms deal damage, rain dims your fire, cold forces you to seek warmth.

Day & Night Cycle

A full day-night cycle with dynamic lighting. Bring a torch or lantern for nighttime exploration — it gets dark out there.

Underground Caves

Descend into pitch-dark caves to mine iron ore, crystal, and rare ore. Bring light — and find your way back out.

Camp Progression

Three camp levels from basic survival to a full log cabin with kitchen, smithing station, smoker, and weather vane. 15+ buildable structures.

Controller Support

Best played with a PlayStation DualSense controller, but fully playable with keyboard and mouse. All controls are documented in the play guide.

New Game+

Complete your journey and start fresh in a new world. Carry 5 items of your choice into the next playthrough — choose wisely.

Procedural Everything

Every tree, rock, animal, cave, and structure is procedurally generated from code. No imported 3D models — just pure geometry with a retro blocky aesthetic.
