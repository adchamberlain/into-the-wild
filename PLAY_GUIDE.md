# Into the Wild — Play Guide

A solo camping adventure game. Survive the wilderness, build your camp, craft tools, and explore a vast procedural world.

---

## Recommended Setup

**We recommend playing with a PlayStation DualSense controller** for the best experience. However, both keyboard/mouse and controller are fully supported — use whichever you prefer.

## Getting Started

You wake up at a campsite in a forest clearing, near a small fishing pond. Your first goal: survive.

### Day 1 Priorities

1. **Gather branches and rocks** — Pick up branches from the ground and river rocks near water. These are your basic crafting materials.
2. **Craft a campfire kit** — You need 4 branches and 3 river rocks. Place it at your campsite for warmth and cooking.
3. **Craft a basic axe** — A stone axe (2 river rocks + 1 branch) lets you chop trees for wood.
4. **Eat what you find** — Berries, mushrooms, and herbs grow in the forest. Eat them raw to stave off hunger.
5. **Build a shelter** — Weather can turn dangerous. A basic shelter protects you from storms.

### First Few Days

- **Craft a fishing rod** (3 branches + 1 rope) at your crafting bench. Fish are one of the best early food sources.
- **Cook your food** — Cooked fish restores 40 hunger vs. 25 raw. Use the fire pit.
- **Build a drying rack** — Preserve food so it doesn't go to waste.
- **Explore carefully** — Stay near camp until you have tools and food stockpiled.

## Survival

### Stats to Manage

| Stat | What Happens at Zero | How to Restore |
|------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Hunger** | Health starts draining (1 pt/sec) | Eat food |
| **Health** | You collapse and respawn at camp, losing some items | Healing salves, herb tea, osha root, or let it regenerate when well-fed |

- Hunger drains faster when sprinting, during heat waves, and in the desert.
- Health regenerates slowly when your hunger is nearly full.
- Storms drain health if you're not in shelter. Cold snaps drain health if you're not near fire.

### Food Progression

| Stage | Best Foods | Hunger Restored |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| Early | Raw berries, mushrooms, raw fish | 10–25 |
| Mid | Cooked fish, cooked meat, berry pouch | 25–40 |
| Late | Hearty stew, smoked fish, mushroom soup | 40–100 |

Some foods also restore health: herb tea (+30 health), hearty stew (+20 health), mushroom soup (+10 health).

## Camp Progression

Your campsite grows through three levels. Each level unlocks new structures and recipes.

### Level 1 — Survival Camp

You start here. Build the basics: fire pit, shelter, crafting bench, drying rack, and craft a fishing rod to unlock Level 2.

### Level 2 — Functional Camp

Unlocks canvas tent, garden plots, snare traps, storage containers, and advanced tools like the bow and grappling hook. Build a tent, storage, garden, and accumulate 6+ structures while surviving 3+ days to unlock Level 3.

### Level 3 — Wilderness Basecamp

The full experience. Build a log cabin with a kitchen for advanced meals (hearty stew restores 100 hunger + 20 health). Unlock the smithing station, smoker, weather vane, and high-tier equipment like diamond tools and the hang glider.

## Exploring the World

The world is procedurally generated with six biomes, each with unique terrain, resources, and challenges.

### Biomes

| Biome | Terrain | Key Resources | Difficulty |
|-------|---------|---------------|------------|
| **Forest** | Dense trees, rolling hills | Wood, branches, berries, herbs | Easy |
| **Meadow** | Open grassland, lakes | Berries, rocks, scattered trees | Easy |
| **Hills** | Dramatic terraces | Mixed resources, some rare ore | Medium |
| **Desert** | Sand dunes, sparse | Cactus fruit, hidden gems | Medium |
| **Rocky** | Jagged cliffs, exposed rock | Crystal, rare ore, abundant stone | Hard |
| **Mountain** | Alpine peaks | Osha root (medicinal), rare ore | Very Hard |

### Caves

2–3 cave entrances spawn across the world, at least 85 units from your campsite. Caves contain valuable resources — iron ore, crystal, and rare ore — but are completely dark inside. **Bring a torch or lantern.**

### Points of Interest

- **Fishing pond**: Always near your campsite (close to spawn)
- **Rivers and lakes**: Scattered across the map, good for fishing and rock gathering
- **Desert sinkhole**: Hidden location containing the Explorer's Journal (+5 max health)
- **Trail landmarks**: Carved trees, stone cairns, and signposts mark paths for navigation

## Hunting & Fishing

### Hunting

Craft a bow and arrows to hunt wildlife:

| Animal | Loot | Found In |
|--------|------|----------|
| Rabbit | 1 Raw Meat, 1 Hide | Forest |
| Bird | 1 Feather | Forest, Hills |

Lizards and tortoises (desert) are decorative — they can't be hunted.

### Fishing

1. Craft a fishing rod (3 Branch + 1 Rope)
2. Find a fishing spot near water
3. Interact (E / L2) to cast your line
4. Wait for a bite (3–8 seconds)
5. Press interact again within the catch window (2 seconds) to reel in

Fish are most active at dawn and morning. Fishing spots temporarily deplete after heavy use but respawn after several game hours.

## Weather

Weather changes each dawn and can dramatically affect gameplay.

| Weather | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Clear** | Normal conditions |
| **Rain** | Fire efficiency reduced, fishing improved |
| **Storm** | Seek shelter or take damage — fires can extinguish |
| **Fog** | Reduced visibility |
| **Heat Wave** | Hunger drains 2x faster |
| **Cold Snap** | Stay near fire or take damage |

Build a **weather vane** (Level 3) to get forecasts and plan accordingly.

---

## Day & Night

One game day lasts about 20 real-world minutes (adjustable in the config menu via Tab / L3+R3).

- **Dawn to evening** (5am–7pm): Safe to explore, good visibility
- **Night** (9pm–5am): Complete darkness away from light sources — bring a torch

The game starts at 8:00am.

## Tools & Equipment

### Axe Progression

| Tool | Speed | How to Get |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Primitive Axe | 0.5x | Hand craft (1 rock + 1 branch) |
| Stone Axe | 1.0x | Hand craft (2 rock + 1 branch) |
| Metal Axe | 2.0x | Bench Level 3 (2 metal ingot + 2 branch) |
| Diamond Axe | 3.0x | Bench Level 3 (2 diamond + 1 metal ingot + 1 rope) |

### Special Tools

- **Grappling Hook** — Climb cliffs and vertical terrain. Essential for reaching caves and mountain peaks. Aim and fire with R2/right-click.
- **Hang Glider** — Soar across the wilderness. Requires Level 3 camp and the Explorer's Journal (found in the desert sinkhole).
- **Map** — Track your explored territory.
- **Compass** — Navigate with direction + place a lodestone beacon at your camp.

## Saving & Loading

Open the **config menu** (Tab on keyboard, or L3+R3 on controller) to access save and load. The game has 5 save slots. Press K to save or L to load while the menu is open.

The game also auto-saves to slot 5 after certain events (like respawning).

---

## New Game Plus

After reaching Level 3 and completing your journey, you'll transition to a new scene. You can then start a **New Game Plus** — a fresh world with a new terrain layout. You get to bring **5 items** of your choice from your previous journey into the new world. Everything else resets: camp level, structures, and world seed.

Choose your 5 items wisely. A metal axe or grappling hook can give you a huge head start.

## Tips

- **Cook everything** — Cooked food restores significantly more hunger than raw.
- **Stockpile rope** — You'll need it for almost every mid-to-late game recipe.
- **Head to the mountains for iron ore** — Smelt it into metal ingots to unlock the best tools and the grappling hook. Caves have crystal and rare ore for late-game gear.
- **Place multiple storage boxes** — Keep resources organized near your crafting bench.
- **Watch the weather** — Don't venture far when storms are coming.
- **Diamond arrows are recoverable** — Worth the investment for hunting.
- **Dried food doesn't spoil** — Build a drying rack early for reliable food stores.
- **The fishing pond is always near camp** — A reliable early food source.
